## 🧭 About Me

<pre><code>name:        Bui The Dung (The Dung Bui)
affiliation: VNU University of Engineering and Technology (VNU-UET)
major:       Information Systems (FIT - UET - VNU)
lab:         SAT Lab @ VNU-UET
focus:       Propositional Satisfiability (SAT / MaxSAT) for Scheduling
motto:       "Every conflict is just a learned clause pointing closer to the solution."</code></pre>

- 🎓 Undergraduate student in **Information Systems** at **VNU-UET** (Hanoi, Vietnam).
- 🔬 Research member at **SAT Lab (VNU-UET)**, working on **SAT, MaxSAT, and CP-SAT encodings** for NP-hard **scheduling** and combinatorial optimization problems.
- 🧪 Committed to transparent, reproducible experiments—sharing both working solvers and **negative results** so every run deepens the understanding.
- 📬 Contact me at **dungbt.research@gmail.com** or check my papers on **[Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=FVmThvgAAAAJ&hl=vi)**.
